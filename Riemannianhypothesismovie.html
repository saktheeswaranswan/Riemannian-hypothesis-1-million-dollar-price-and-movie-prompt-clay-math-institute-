Canonical equations and fully substituted expansion

1. Riemann zeta function

ζ(s) = Σn=1∞ n−s,   Re(s) > 1

ρk = ½ + iγk

The ordinates γk are numerical approximations to the imaginary parts of the selected non-trivial zeros. The global Riemann Hypothesis remains unproved.

2. Exact Chebyshev function

ψ(x) = Σn≤x Λ(n) = Σpm≤x log p

Λ(n) = log p when n is a positive power of a prime p, and zero otherwise.

3. Explicit formula structure

ψ(x) = x − Σρ xρ/ρ − log(2π) − ½ log(1 − x−2)
